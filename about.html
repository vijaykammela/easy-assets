About

Built to get out of your way

EasyAssets started as a personal folder of textures and blocks collected over years of studio work, cleaned up and put online so other architects, designers, and students could skip the part where you dig through five sketchy sites and three pop-up ads just to find one decent brick texture.

There's no account system here, and there won't be. Every file on this site downloads directly — click, save, done. That's the whole product.

What you'll find

  • Seamless, tileable textures for rendering — wood, stone, concrete, metal, and fabric.
  • CAD blocks in DWG format, drawn to real-world scale for plan and elevation work.
  • SketchUp models ready to drop into a scene without cleanup.

How it stays free

The site is supported by a small amount of unobtrusive advertising — you'll notice a few clearly marked ad slots around the page, always kept well away from download buttons. No file is ever hidden behind a paywall or a forced click-through.

New assets, added by hand

Every asset on this site is added manually, in small batches, as time allows. If there's a specific texture, block, or model you're looking for, get in touch — requests genuinely do shape what gets added next.
